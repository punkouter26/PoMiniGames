Finding · TTFT / tokens-per-sec are not measured

No AI consumer wraps its ChatClient call with a token-counting IChatClient decorator or a TTFT timer. OpenTelemetry spans are emitted by the Azure SDK but no report (server or client) reads them. The prompt's requirement to plot TTFT and tokens/sec is therefore a gap finding, not a chart.

3. Anthropic — explicitly absent

A grep across src/**/*.cs for Anthropic|claude|haiku|sonnet|opus returns one hit: the literal word "Sonnet" inside a PoFunQuiz trivia question bank ("Shakespeare wrote 'Romeo and Juliet'. What type of work is it?", answer index 3). There is no AnthropicClient, no model alias, no AnthropicOptions, and no PoShared contract for Anthropic. The conclusion is unambiguous: PoMiniGames does not integrate Anthropic today.

Why this matters

If the prompt expects a Claude-backed rewrite/judge/explain path in any of these endpoints, that capability does not exist and must be added before any "Anthropic fallback" claim is made in product material.

4. Cost projection — labelled assumption, not a quote

PoMiniGames:AI:FoundryEndpoint and PoMiniGames:AI:DefaultDeployment are configured. No per-token prices are configured anywhere in appsettings*.json; no AiPricing section exists. The projections below multiply three assumed volumes by three labelled tiers and present zero as the price column.

Finding · No per-token pricing is configured

Per the prompt's contract, this report does not fabricate numbers. The bars above are zero, and the table's third column is the public-retail estimate shown only so the bars have something to compare against — not a quote the app would bill against. To replace these with real cost data, add a PoMiniGames:AI:Pricing config section and have ConfigurationDiagnosticsSnapshotProvider project it through /api/diag.

5. Centralization contract

One AzureOpenAIClient + per-deployment ChatClient cache, shared by every AI consumer. Per-game secrets live only inside AIFoundryOptions and PoMiniGames:AI:FoundryEndpoint / DefaultDeployment in Key Vault.
